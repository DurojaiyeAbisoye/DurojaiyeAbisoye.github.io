About

I'm Abisoye Emmanuel Durojaiye, a Data Scientist and Machine Learning Engineer with a focus on building systems that are both analytically rigorous and practically useful.

My work spans the full data lifecycle — from cleaning and exploring data, to training and deploying models that actually hold up in production. I'm particularly drawn to problems that sit at the edge of what current methods handle well.

Outside of work, I write occasionally about theory and code — trying to make ideas that feel abstract a little more concrete.
